HOW TO READ THIS DECK

The Math Behind It

Every formula starts as a plain question. We try something simple, watch it fail, fix it, and only then does the formula make sense — instead of appearing out of nowhere.
01 / MEAN

Why x̄ = (1/n)Σxᵢ

It's the unique number that minimizes total squared distance to every point — not a convention, a consequence of calculus.
02 / MEDIAN

Why "sort, then take the middle"

Minimizing total absolute distance (not squared) is what makes the median ignore outliers.
03 / MODE

Why mode = argmax freq(v)

argmax returns the input that wins, not the score it won with — a distinction worth getting right early.
04 / VARIANCE

Why σ² = (1/n)Σ(xᵢ−x̄)²

Plain deviations cancel to zero. Absolute value fixes that but isn't smooth. Squaring fixes both problems at once.
05 / STANDARD DEVIATION

Why σ = √σ²

The square root exists for exactly one reason: variance is in squared units, and nobody thinks in dollars².
06 / COVARIANCE

Why cov(X,Y) = (1/n)Σ(xᵢ−x̄)(yᵢ−ȳ)

Multiplying two deviations is a sign-detector: same-direction pairs add positive, opposite pairs add negative.
07 / CORRELATION

Why r = cov(X,Y) / (σx·σy)

Dividing by both spreads cancels units — and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality is the actual proof r can't leave [−1, 1].
08 / PERCENTILES

Why rank = ⌈(p/100)·n⌉

Turn a percentage into a position: what fraction of the data, how many points that covers, then round up to a whole spot.
09 / CONFIDENCE INTERVAL

Why x̄ ± z*·(σ/√n)

σ/√n is how much the sample mean itself typically wobbles — that wobble, scaled by how sure you want to be, is the margin.
10 / HYPOTHESIS TESTING

Why z = (x̄−μ₀) / (σ/√n)

Same standard error as the confidence interval — reused to ask a different question: how many "typical wobbles" away did we land?
11 / P-VALUE

Why p = tail area beyond z

Not a yes/no cutoff — an actual probability: how likely is data this extreme, purely by chance, if H₀ were true?
12 / ANOVA

Why F = MS_between / MS_within

Total variation splits cleanly into "between groups" (signal) and "within groups" (noise) — F is just their ratio.
